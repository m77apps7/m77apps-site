Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone apps Punfortunate and Ring Box, made by M77 Apps ("we", "us"). The short version: we don't collect personal information.

At a glance
  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Nothing you type or create in the apps is sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Punfortunate

Jokes and settings downloads

Punfortunate downloads joke packs and app settings from Apple's iCloud service (a public CloudKit database that we publish to). These downloads are read-only. The app does not upload anything about you, and we cannot see who downloads what.

On-device identifier

The first time it runs, the app creates a random identifier and keeps it on your device. It is used only on the device to choose between versions of a feature, for example how many free jokes you get. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account or any other information, and it never leaves your device. Because it is stored in the device keychain, it may remain on the device after you delete the app.

Voice and sound

Spoken jokes use recorded audio bundled with the app or the speech voices built into iOS. Speech is generated on the device. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them, including items shared through Family Sharing. We don't receive your name, email or payment information.

Ring Box

Ring Box works entirely on your device and makes no network connections. The ring design and the optional inscription you enter are saved only on your device. The app reads the phone's motion sensors in real time for visual effects and does not store or send that data. It does not use the microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location.

Information Apple provides to us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics, such as download counts and sales, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers in their iPhone settings. This information does not identify you to us. Apple's own handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Both apps are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. This applies to residents of every region, including California and the European Union.

Deleting your data

Deleting an app removes the data it stored on your device, except the random identifier described above, which contains no personal information. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored at any time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If an app ever starts collecting personal information, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before that version is released.
