Punfortunate Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app Punfortunate, made by M77 Apps ("we", "us"). The short version: we don't collect personal information. The app sends a few anonymous usage counts so we can make the jokes better, and you can turn them off.

At a glance
  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Anonymous usage counts only, never anything about you. You can turn them off on the Shelf.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Jokes and settings downloads

Punfortunate downloads joke packs and app settings from Apple's iCloud service (a public CloudKit database that we publish to). These downloads are read-only, and we cannot see who downloads what.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which jokes land and how the app is used, Punfortunate sends a few small counts to the same iCloud database, for example "the app was opened today", "5 jokes were told in this visit", "the free jokes ran out", "an item was bought", and which jokes were finished, cut off or saved as a favorite.

Each count carries only the app version, the day (no time), the type of iPhone (with or without a hinge), which version of a feature you are trying, rough numbers such as how many days since the app was installed, and, if a joke download fails, the reason it failed.

The counts contain no name, Apple Account, email, device identifier, advertising identifier, location or IP address.

iCloud itself adds a random code to every record saved in an app's iCloud database, the same code for everything sent from one iCloud account to Punfortunate. Apple creates it; it does not reveal your name or Apple Account and is different in every app. We never use it to group counts or look anyone up.

Counts are sent only when your iPhone is signed in to iCloud. To stop them, turn off "Share anonymous usage" on the Shelf (the bag icon, My jokes tab); anything not yet sent is deleted.

On-device identifier

The first time it runs, the app creates a random identifier and keeps it on your device. It is used only on the device to choose between versions of a feature, for example how many free jokes you get. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account or any other information, and it never leaves your device. Because it is stored in the device keychain, it may remain on the device after you delete the app.

Voice and sound

Spoken jokes use recorded audio bundled with the app or the speech voices built into iOS. Speech is generated on the device. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them, including items shared through Family Sharing. We don't receive your name, email or payment information.

Information Apple provides to us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics, such as download counts and sales, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers in their iPhone settings. This information does not identify you to us. Apple's own handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Punfortunate is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage counts described above contain nothing that identifies a person.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. This applies to residents of every region, including California and the European Union.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your device, except the random identifier described above, which contains no personal information. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored at any time. Anonymous counts already sent can't be traced back to you, so they can't be found or deleted per person.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If Punfortunate ever starts collecting personal information, we will update this policy and its App Store privacy details before that version is released.
