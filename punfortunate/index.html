M77 Apps · Help & support

Punfortunate

A hinge puppet that tells dad jokes. Open the phone for the setup, wider for the punchline, then send the joke to someone who deserves it.

Contact

Email support@m77apps.com and we'll reply within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. Or open the Shelf (the bag icon), Shop tab, and tap Report a Problem: it starts the email for you with a short support code that tells us your app version and what you own, nothing about you.

On the App Store, Punfortunate is sold by Metin Demircioglu, founder of M77 Studios LLC, until the developer account moves to the company.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. Punfortunate runs on any iPhone. On iPhone Duo, hold the phone sideways and the hinge works the puppet's mouth. On other iPhones, pull his chin down for a joke and further for the punchline.

How do I share a joke?

After the punchline, tap Share next to the heart. Punfortunate makes a short video of Dad telling the joke, right on your phone, and opens the share sheet so you can send it or save it. Jokes you keep on the Shelf have Share too. During the free week, sending one brings tomorrow's free jokes forward to today, once a day.

I can't hear the jokes

Turn up the volume and check that the Ring/Silent switch or Action button isn't set to silent. If you use Bluetooth headphones, make sure they're connected.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap the bag icon to open the Shelf, then tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases also work on your other devices and, where Family Sharing is on, for your family.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the purchase.

What data does Punfortunate collect?

No personal information. The app sends a few anonymous usage counts (like how many jokes were told), which you can turn off on the Shelf. See the Punfortunate Privacy Policy.
