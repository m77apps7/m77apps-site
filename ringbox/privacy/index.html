The Ring Box Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone app The Ring Box, made by M77 Studios LLC, doing business as M77 Apps ("M77 Apps", "we", "us"). The short version: we don't collect personal information, and the app uses the internet only to reach Apple's App Store for its optional in-app purchase.

At a glance
  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Your ring design and inscription are saved on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • The app's only network use is Apple's App Store, for the optional in-app purchase.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

The ring design and the optional inscription you enter are saved on your device, along with your settings. Your device backups may include them, depending on your Apple backup settings; we have no access to those backups. The app reads the phone's motion sensors in real time for visual effects and does not store or send that data. It does not use the microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app contacts the App Store to show the price, make or restore a purchase, and check whether you own the customization unlock. We don't receive your name, email or payment information.

Information Apple provides to us

Apple gives developers aggregated App Store statistics, such as download counts and sales, and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers in their iPhone settings. This information does not identify you to us. Apple's own handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The Ring Box is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. This applies to residents of every region, including California and the European Union.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Backup copies are managed in your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not cancel or refund a purchase, and you can restore it at any time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If The Ring Box ever starts collecting personal information, we will update this policy and its App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@m77apps.com
