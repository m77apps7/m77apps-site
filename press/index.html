Punfortunate press kit

A dad joke puppet for iPhone. On iPhone Duo, the hinge is his mouth.

Out October 23, 2026, the day iPhone Duo goes on sale. Free on the App Store, with in-app purchases.

Punfortunate turns your phone into a dad joke puppet. On iPhone Duo, open the phone and Dad sets up the joke. He pauses. Open wider and he delivers the punchline, then grins like it's the best one he's ever told. Fold the phone mid-joke and he goes quiet. Open it again and he'll let you know: tough crowd. On any other iPhone, pull his chin down to work his jaw, so everyone can play along.

What's in the app

  • A puppet with perfect comic timing and no shame
  • Free starter jokes, then a few free jokes a day for a week
  • Reloads: fresh packs of jokes you haven't heard. Unlimited Reloads: every reload, now and later, in one purchase
  • Deluxe Dad: Dad in 3D
  • Tap the heart to keep a joke and replay it from the Shelf
  • Share any joke as a short video, told by Dad himself
  • Turn on Show jokes only once and Dad never repeats himself
  • Works offline. Unlimited Reloads and Deluxe Dad work with Family Sharing

From the founder
