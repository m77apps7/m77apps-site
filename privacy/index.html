Website Privacy

Effective November 13, 2026

This page covers the website m77apps.com, including its short links (m77apps.com/go/…), run by M77 Studios LLC, doing business as M77 Apps ("M77 Apps", "we", "us"). Our apps have their own privacy policies: Punfortunate and The Ring Box.

At a glance
  • We count visits to learn which pages and links get used. That's all.
  • No cookies, no ads, no tracking across websites.
  • We never see or store your IP address or anything that identifies you.

What we count

When you open a page on this site, or tap one of our short links, your browser sends a small count to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter. We see totals only, for example "120 visits to the Punfortunate page this week" or "40 taps on the Instagram link".

Along with the page or short link, the totals show where a visit came from (the referring site, when your browser shares it), the browser and system type (such as "Safari on iOS"), the screen width, the language and the country. The country is worked out from your IP address while the count is made, and the IP address itself is not stored.

What we don't do

The counter sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It doesn't keep your IP address, your full browser details or any ID that could follow you from one site to another. We don't record individual visits, only totals. We don't use the counts for advertising and don't combine them with any other data.

To avoid counting one visit twice, GoatCounter briefly keeps a combination of your IP address and browser details in memory. That is never written to disk and is gone after at most 8 hours.

Who handles the counts

GoatCounter is run by Martin Tournoij in Ireland and stores the counts on servers in Finland and Germany. It doesn't share them with anyone else. Its own privacy policy has the details. We keep the totals for as long as they help us improve the site, and can delete them at any time.

Short links

Our short links (m77apps.com/go/…) send you straight on to their destination, usually the App Store. The count is made on the way. Once you arrive, the destination's own privacy policy applies, for example Apple's Privacy Policy on the App Store.

Blocking the count

Most content blockers stop the counter, and the site works the same either way.

Changes

If we change how the website counts visits, we will update this page and the effective date above.
