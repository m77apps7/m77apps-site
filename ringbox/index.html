M77 Apps · Help & support

The Ring Box

A digital ring box for proposals. Add an inscription, close the box, and open it at the right moment: tap it on iPhone, or open the phone itself on iPhone Duo.

Contact

Email support@m77apps.com and we'll reply within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. The Ring Box works on any iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Tap Ready to show the closed box, then tap the box to open the lid. On iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later, the phone's hinge is the lid: close the phone after tapping Ready, then open it slowly.

I can't hear the fanfare

Turn up the volume and check that the Ring/Silent switch or Action button isn't set to silent.

Is my inscription private?

Yes. Your ring design and inscription stay on your phone and are never uploaded. See The Ring Box Privacy Policy.
